Block Marchers · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Block Marchers ("the game") is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited, Rajkot, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy covers the game on iPhone, iPad and Android. It says what the game does today and what we plan to add before it reaches the App Store and Google Play. Each part is marked TODAY or PLANNED.

The short version

  • TODAY The game collects nothing. It does not connect to the internet, and nothing you do in it is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • There is no account and no sign-in, now or later, and we will never sell personal data. Analytics are not planned; if we ever add any, they will be opt-in only and we will update this policy first.
  • PLANNED When the game is released, it will show ads (non-personalised only), sell optional items such as Remove Ads, offer leaderboards, and keep a copy of your progress in your own iCloud or Google Play Games account. Each of these is described below. We will update this policy before any of them reaches the game.

What stays on your device TODAY

The game keeps the following on your phone or tablet only: your best March score and number of runs, the stars you won on each Job, your best Daily Job result for the day, and your settings (sound, haptics, controls, button labels, left-handed layout, Guide, reduced motion, pace). There is no name, email or other personal detail in it.

  • The Daily Job is chosen from today's date on your device. Nothing is downloaded.
  • On iPhone and iPad, your device's own iCloud Backup (if you use it) may include this data, so it can return on a new device. Apple handles that backup under your account; we cannot see it.
  • On Android, the game's data is not included in Google backups or device transfers.
  • Deleting the game removes all of it from the device.

Your choices PLANNED

  • Ads are never shown during play, on launch or when you leave. Rewarded ads are always your choice.
  • All ads are non-personalised. On iPhone and iPad the game never asks to track you.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the game shows Google's consent form first, and Settings > Privacy choices opens it again.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Remove Ads, a one-time purchase, turns off the ads between runs.
  • Settings > Delete my data removes your progress from the device and the copy in your iCloud or Play Games account. Purchases stay with your store account and come back with Restore Purchases.

Children and age

The game is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

PLANNED Where the law requires it (for example in some US states), the game will read the age range your App Store or Google Play account shares, on the device only. Ads are already non-personalised for everyone; for players under 18, purchases follow the store's parental approval, and for players under 13 the share card is turned off. The age range is never stored or sent to us. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

Players in India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

  • Encircle Technologies Private Limited is the Data Fiduciary for the game. TODAY the game processes no personal data on our side, so no notice or consent request is shown.
  • PLANNED In India, as everywhere, ads will be non-personalised only and there will be no analytics. If the store shares no age, the game treats you with these safe settings and does not ask your age. Your progress will be kept only on your device and in your own iCloud or Play Games account.
  • PLANNED Before the game asks for any consent, it will show a short notice in English or Hindi: what is used, why, how to change your mind and how to complain.
  • You can ask us for a summary of the data we hold about you and the services that process it, ask us to correct or erase it, and name another person to act for you. Email our grievance contact, support@encircletechnologies.com. We aim to reply within 7 days and reply within 30 days at most.
  • Some planned services handle data outside India (Google, RevenueCat and Apple, in their own locations).
  • If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
  • This policy is available on request in Hindi and the other languages in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution.

Security and retention

Data on your device stays there until you delete the game or use Delete my data. Data handled by the planned services is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to their policies. We do not sell personal information.

Contact and requests

For questions, data requests or complaints, email support@encircletechnologies.com. We reply within 30 days at most (7 days is our aim). Encircle Technologies Private Limited, Rajkot, Gujarat, India.

Changes
