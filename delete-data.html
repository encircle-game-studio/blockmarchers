Delete your Block Marchers data

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Block Marchers is made by Encircle Technologies Private Limited. There is no Block Marchers account: your progress lives on your device and, once cloud save arrives, in your own iCloud or Google Play Games account. This page explains how to delete it, and how to ask us to delete anything held for us. Parts marked PLANNED apply when that feature reaches the game.

Today: delete the game TODAY

The current version keeps everything on your device and sends nothing to us.

  • iPhone and iPad: delete the app. This removes your progress, stars and settings. If you use iCloud Backup, an older backup of your device may still hold a copy; you can delete old backups in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Backups.
  • Android: uninstall Block Marchers. This removes everything; the game's data is not included in Google backups.

After the update with cloud save PLANNED

Open the game, go to Settings › Privacy › Delete my data and confirm. This removes your progress and settings from the device and from the copy in your iCloud or Play Games account. Purchases stay with your store account; use Restore Purchases to get them back.

Uninstalling the game does not remove the copy in your iCloud or Play Games account. Use Delete my data first, or remove the game's data from your account (Play Games profile, or iCloud settings on your iPhone).

Ask us to delete the data held for us

  1. Email support@encircletechnologies.com with the subject "Delete my Block Marchers data".
  2. Say which platform you play on (Android or iOS).
  3. If you bought anything, include the order number from your Google Play or Apple receipt so we can find the matching purchase record.

Today we hold no data about you, and we will confirm that in writing if you ask. We reply within 30 days and confirm when a deletion is done.

Advertising and leaderboard data PLANNED

  • Ads: Google AdMob handles ad data. On Android, delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads). On iPhone and iPad the game does not use the advertising identifier. Google's ad controls are at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Leaderboards and achievements: these are kept by Apple Game Center or Google Play Games under your own account. Manage or delete them in your Play Games profile (play.google.com/games/profile) or through Apple's Data and Privacy page (privacy.apple.com).
