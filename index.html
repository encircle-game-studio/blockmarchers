Block Marchers

Build the road home. A retro falling block puzzle where every block you drop is ground for a crowd of little Marchers. They climb one-block steps, turn at walls and drop down gaps. Build stairs and bridges to get them to the Door, and clear full rows before your stack hits the top. A short lesson teaches the moves, and you choose Touch or Buttons controls.
